CHENNAI HERITAGE

A Legacy of Spices & Taste

Topi Vappa Biryani has been synonymous with the rich culinary landscape of Chennai. Built on generations of authentic spice blends, secret family recipes, and the traditional slow-cooking dum method.

Our signature handi pots are packed with aged long-grain basmati rice, tender pieces of handpicked cuts of meat, and rare spices sourced directly from local farmers in Southern India. We seal our pots with dough to lock in the steam, creating the legendary, rich aroma that hits you the moment the lid is opened.
